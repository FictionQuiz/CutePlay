A LITTLE HELLO

Made for happy little moments. ♡

CutePlay is a playful corner for quick quizzes, silly games and friendship challenges. Make a quiz about your favourite things, share it with friends and see how well they know you.

How the Bestie Quiz works

  1. Create a quiz and write your own questions.
  2. Choose four answer options and mark the correct answer.
  3. Share your unique link with friends.
  4. Friends answer the questions and receive a score based on the answers you selected.
  5. They can pass the challenge along or create their own quiz.

Keep it kind

Results are for entertainment. Please avoid adding private, identifying or sensitive information to quizzes. Share links only with people you trust.
