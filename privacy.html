THE IMPORTANT LITTLE DETAILS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

CutePlay is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Firebase services to store user-created quizzes and scoreboard entries. Players are signed in through Firebase Anonymous Authentication; no email address or password is requested by CutePlay for this feature.

Quiz links and stored quizzes

When you create a quiz, its nickname, questions, answer choices and correct-answer settings are stored in Firebase Firestore. The site creates a short link using a random document ID. Anyone who has that link may be able to view and play the quiz. Do not include passwords, contact details, addresses, private messages or other sensitive information.

Scoreboard

If you choose to save a result, your nickname, quiz name, score, total questions and percentage are stored in Firestore and displayed on the shared CutePlay scoreboard. Use a nickname rather than your full name. Score entries are public to visitors of the scoreboard.

Browser storage

The sound preference may be saved in your browser using local storage. It is used only to remember whether sound is on or off.

Hosting and third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Google Fonts may be requested to display the site's typeface. Quiz answer pictures are loaded from images hosted by Unsplash. When a browser requests these images, the image host may receive technical information such as the browser's IP address. These services may process information under their own privacy terms. Analytics and advertising integrations should be disclosed here before they are enabled.

Children and personal information

Use nicknames rather than full names and do not publish personal or sensitive information. Ask a trusted adult if you are unsure whether something is safe to share.

Changes

CutePlay uses Google Firebase Analytics, where supported, to understand general site usage. Firebase and Google may process technical information such as device, browser and usage events under their own privacy terms. This policy should be reviewed whenever the site adds or changes analytics, advertising, accounts, data storage or other third-party services.
